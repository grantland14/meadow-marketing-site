questions, answered softly

Gentle answers

What counts as movement?

Anything that moves you. A walk, a swim, a stretch, gardening, dancing while the pasta boils. Meadow never tells you how to move — it just makes the movement you already love count. There's no exercise content, no workouts to follow, and nothing to keep up with.

What happens if I miss a day?

Nothing. Truly nothing. Your flowers stay, your day count stays, and tomorrow is simply the next day you show up. There are no streaks in Meadow, so there is nothing to break — the worst possible day is "the meadow waited."

What if I only have a few minutes?

They count. On low-energy days, Meadow gently suggests less and tells you the truth: ten gentle minutes is a win. Even ninety seconds keeps your meadow growing — the bar stays on the floor so the habit survives your hardest days. That's exactly when it matters most.

How much does Meadow cost?

Meadow is $9.99 a month or $39.99 a year, and your first week is free — so a few flowers can bloom before you decide. One subscription covers everything: every feature, every seed packet, every visitor. Cancel anytime; your meadow stays.

Does it work with Apple Health?

It does. Connect once, and your walks and workouts grow the meadow automatically — no extra logging needed.

Is this a fitness app?

Not really. Meadow is a feeling-better app. There are no workout plans, no calorie counts, and no numbers that judge you. Just one gentle thing a day, a growing meadow, and quiet proof — from your own life — that moving helps.
